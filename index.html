The Mission
What Is F3?

F3 — Fitness, Fellowship, Faith — traces its roots to a free, participant-led boot camp held on Saturday mornings in Charlotte, NC. What started as a handful of guys has grown into a nationwide movement of men committed to getting better together.

F3 Snacktown is one of the strongest regions in the nation, spanning across Gettysburg, Spring Grove, Westminster, and of course Hanover, PA. We have 10+ workout locations, and workouts 6 days a week — all completely free.

The workouts are intense but scalable. The community is the reason men stay.

"Leave no man behind, but leave no man where you found him."

PAX = participants. Q = the workout leader. FNG = Friendly New Guy (first timer). EH = Emotional Headlock (how you convince a buddy to come). AO = Area of Operations (workout location). HC = Hard Commit (you said you’d be there, so you’re there). COT = Circle of Trust (closing circle).
